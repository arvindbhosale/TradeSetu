# Changelog

All notable changes to TradeSetu are documented here.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Fixed
- **Intraday backtests and algos no longer buy on yesterday's signal.** With your own Buy when / Sell when rules, a buy signal stayed "on" after the square-off until the Sell rule fired, so the next morning the backtest (and the intraday algo) bought at the first candle of the window even when none of the Buy rules held that day. A signal from an earlier day is now bought only when today's candles meet the Buy rules again. Positional trades and built-in strategies are unchanged (`Strategy.entries`, `entry_signal`; algo status **Running: waiting for today's buy signal**).

### Added
- **Backtest results in Excel.** **Download in Excel** on the Backtest page saves the result on screen (stocks, buying index options, option strategies, index futures, ETF portfolios) as a workbook: Summary (every tile, benchmark comparison, trust checks as worded on the page, steps/adds comparison), native Excel charts (portfolio value, drawdown, monthly and yearly profit/loss, each trade), every trade with a running total, monthly and yearly returns with a year-by-month grid, the daily equity curve and every setting (`desk/btexport.py`, `POST /api/backtest/export.xlsx`). Downloads now also work in the phone app: the file is handed to the phone's browser through a five-minute one-off link (`POST /api/download/handoff`, `GET /dl/<token>`), which also fixes the P&L, journal, tax and AI scan downloads there.
- **Backtests test what algos do.** Index futures and options buying backtests take **Maximum loss (₹)** and **Target profit (₹)** for the position, as F&O algos have them (a live algo needs a maximum loss), and **Create algo** carries them over. F&O algos take the backtests' whole-position **Stoploss / Target %** (an option strategy's % of its premium, a future's % of its price), so the % exits a backtest tested are no longer dropped when it becomes an algo (`stop_rs`/`target_rs` in `/api/backtest/futures` and `/api/backtest/options`; `stop_pct`/`target_pct` in `/api/fno-algos`).
- **Index strip** along the top of every page (desktop, phone and the apps): NIFTY 50, SENSEX, BANK NIFTY, FIN NIFTY, NIFTY NEXT 50, MIDCAP 100, SMALLCAP 100 and INDIA VIX scrolling with their change, live from your broker (one quote call for the whole strip, at most every 15 seconds while the market is open, every 5 minutes when closed). Badge: Live, Delayed, Market closed, or Offline with the last daily closes. Press an index to open its live chart. Pick the indices or switch it off in Settings → Index strip (`desk/ticker.py`, `GET /api/ticker`, `GET /api/ticker/choices`, settings `ticker_show`, `ticker_indices`).
- **Add to winners** in every backtest (stocks, futures, options buying, option strategies) and every algo (stock and F&O): buy more (% of the first buy's shares, or whole lots) at set profit levels from the first entry, up to 5 adds. Option strategies add one lot of every leg (bought legs first) or lots of one gaining bought leg alone, never a sold leg alone. Each add is checked against the money or margin (Zerodha basket margin where available) and skipped when short; after an add the stoploss of the whole position moves so the loss stays within the first plan (or to breakeven). No adds after a profit step. Backtests show the result with and without adds; adds appear in trades, algo logs and the journal (**Each buy (IST)**) (`desk/scalein.py`; `adds` in backtest, `/api/algos` and `/api/fno-algos` payloads; `adds_compare`).
- **Book profit in steps** in every backtest (stocks, futures, options buying, option strategies) and every algo (stock and F&O): close part of the position at each profit level (% of shares or whole lots), then move the stoploss of the rest to cost or to the previous step. Option strategies close matched lots of every leg, sold legs bought back before hedges; positional stock algos place the GTT again for the shares left. Backtests show the result with and without steps; partial closes appear in trades and the journal (`desk/scaleout.py`; `steps` in backtest, `/api/algos` and `/api/fno-algos` payloads; `steps_compare`, `partials`).
- **Short sell from the live chart** (NSE/BSE, paper and live Kite): an intraday (MIS) short sale with its Stoploss line above the price and Target below, dragged like a buy's; a Short position drawing fills it (**Short sell with this plan**). A short sale counts as a new position for the kill switch, trade window, limit per order, orders-a-day limit and risk limit per trade; buying back never is blocked. Live: one stoploss buy order at Zerodha that the desk turns into a limit buy at the target. Paper: the desk watches the levels and buys back at the last price, and buys back every paper short at the square-off time. An intraday Buy while short buys it back. The ticket's trade type reads **Intraday** / **Positional**; positional is greyed out for a short (Indian exchanges allow no delivery short). Orders for shares take `intent` `open` (SELL) / `close` (BUY) for MIS only; the confirmation phrase of a short is `SHORT SELL <qty> <symbol>`; `short: true` in `POST /api/chart-orders/preview`.
- **Close algo positions yourself**: **Exit selected** (tick algos, stocks and F&O together), **Close all paper** and **Close all live** on the Algos tab, and **Exit** in an algo's Details. Exit now also works on a stopped algo that still holds shares (it only sells). Target/stoploss GTTs are cancelled before the sell; F&O sold legs are bought back before hedges are sold; live exits need typing EXIT (`POST /api/algo-exits` with `mode` or `stock`/`fno` ids).
- **Open and close times** (IST) in the trade journal and in each algo's Details (`closed_at`, `opened_at`/`closed_at` on algo legs, `entry_at` on stock algos); F&O algos list their closed trades with open and close times.
- **Highest and lowest open P&L each day** for every open trade and every algo, sampled once a minute while the desk runs (09:15 to 23:30 IST on trading days), shown in the journal (**Highest open P&L**, day-by-day on press) and in algo Details (`desk/openpeaks.py`, `open_peaks.json`; `daily`, `best_open`, `worst_open` in `GET /api/journal`, `daily` in algo detail).
- **Direct Kite Connect with your own API key and secret** (Settings → Broker → Zerodha Kite Connect). TradeSetu talks to Kite's REST API itself: no Node.js and no Kite MCP server, so the desktop app on Windows can connect, place orders and GTTs, and use Zerodha's basket margin calculator. **One login a day**: the access token is saved in `kite-session.json` (owner-only) and reused across app and server restarts until 6 AM IST, shared by orders, algos, charts, F&O and margin; asked again only after 6 AM, after **Log out of Kite**, or when Zerodha refuses it. The login is checked with a one-time state on `/broker/callback`, and a redirect address can be pasted under **Finish Kite login** (`desk/kiteconnect.py`, setting `kite_connect`, `POST /api/kite/session`, `POST /api/kite/logout`). Without a saved key the MCP server is used as before. The local desk prefers port 47800 so the redirect URL stays `http://127.0.0.1:47800/broker/callback`.
- **Intraday or Positional** trade type for stock backtests, stock algos and the optimiser. Positional on minute or hourly candles holds across days: no trading hours or square-off, delivery (CNC) charges (a same-day round trip at intraday rates), a stoploss or target the price gaps past overnight filled at the next open, and an optional **Sell after holding this many trading days** (Advanced; also for day candles). Positional algos send CNC orders, also place the target and stoploss as a GTT so the broker guards the position overnight, across restarts and before the morning login, move it up with a trailing stoploss once a candle, are not sold by the daily loss limit, and keep their entry day across restarts. Day candles stay positional; requests and saved algos without a trade type keep their old behaviour (`trade_type`, `max_hold_days` in `POST /api/backtest/strategy`, `/api/optimise` and `/api/algos`; `trade_type` in the backtest result).
- **Price alerts** on the P&L & alerts tab: a message when a share, a future, an MCX contract or a coin rises to or falls to a price, to the alert log and Telegram. Each fires once; one set on the wrong side is refused. Indian prices come only from the broker and only while that market is open (NSE/BSE or MCX hours); crypto is checked all day. Checked every 15 seconds on a thread that runs only while an alert waits (`desk/pricealerts.py`, `GET/POST /api/price-alerts`, `DELETE /api/price-alerts/{id}`, `POST /api/price-alerts/clear`).
- **Is the best setting luck?** in the walk-forward optimiser: the probability of backtest overfitting (combinatorially symmetric cross-validation over 10 blocks of every setting's daily returns), and **Every setting at a glance**, a heatmap of the score over two settings (`desk/luck.py`; `luck` and `heatmap` in the optimiser result).
- **Pair trading** backtest (Backtest → More tools): two stocks, a rolling hedge ratio and z-score from past prices only, trades at the next day's open, exit at normal, stoploss gap and time limit, costs on both legs; correlation, half-life of the gap and today's signal; example Indian pairs (`desk/pairs.py`, `GET /api/pairs/options`, `POST /api/pairs/backtest`).
- **Signal test** (Backtest → More tools): ranks every stock of an NSE index (Nifty 500 by default) by one of eight signals every 1 to 63 trading days and reports the rank information coefficient, its t-statistic and hit rate, next returns by group, the top group against all stocks after costs, and today's top 20. Runs in the background with progress and Stop; prices are cached with the AI scan's (`desk/factortest.py`, `POST /api/signal-test`).
- **ML strategy: return forecast (Qlib-style)**. A ridge model on Alpha158-style price and volume features (after Microsoft Qlib, MIT) forecasts the return over the next N days, trained walk-forward (only labels already known, refit every 21 bars on the last N bars, scaling from training rows only); long while the forecast is above a threshold. numpy only, about 25 ms per 6-year backtest. It is an ordinary strategy, so it works in backtests, the AI scan (judged on unseen prices) and algos (`desk/mlsignals.py`, strategy `ml_forecast`).
- **Algo supervisor** (Algos → Supervisor). Watches the running algos and only cuts risk: hold an algo's new entries for the day, close its position and stop it, or turn the kill switch on; it never opens a trade, starts an algo or changes its settings. Fixed rules every minute (a % loss backstop on stock positions, hold every algo past 80% of the daily loss limit, flag F&O algos near their maximum loss and live algos without a loss limit) and an optional AI review in market hours through the AI service in Settings, whose JSON answer is checked against the allowed actions and real algo ids. Modes off / watch / protect; acts on live algos only when allowed; daily cap on AI actions; every step logged with its reason (`desk/supervisor.py`, `GET /api/supervisor`, `POST /api/supervisor/check`, `POST /api/supervisor/review`). Stock algos gain **Exit** (`POST /api/algos/{id}/exit`) and both kinds a hold that **Allow buys/entries** lifts (`POST /api/algos/{id}/release`, `POST /api/fno-algos/{id}/release`).
- Live chart **watchlist, drawings, CSV import and crypto paper trading**. Watchlist with last prices (crypto from the exchange, NSE/BSE from the broker, imports' last close; `GET/PUT /api/md/watchlist`, `GET /api/md/prices`). Trend line, horizontal line and Fibonacci retracement, saved per symbol (`GET/PUT /api/md/drawings`). CSV import of OHLC(V) candles with the candle size read from the file, bad rows reported and files over 10% bad refused (`GET/POST /api/md/imports`, `DELETE /api/md/imports/{symbol}`, `venue=imported`). Crypto paper trading with a virtual USDT balance: market and limit orders, reserved cash and coins, no shorting, 0.1% fee, positions, P&L, history and a reset that deletes the paper data (`desk/cryptopaper.py`, `/api/cpaper`). Paper only; it never places a real order.
- **Live chart** on the Charts tab, with **crypto**. Candles, line or area; 1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, 4h, 1D, 1W; volume; SMA, EMA, Bollinger Bands, RSI, MACD and VWAP computed in the browser so they move with every live price, with adjustable settings. Crypto comes from Binance's public market data and stream, with Coinbase Exchange as the fallback behind one provider interface (`desk/marketdata/`, `GET /api/md/catalog`, `GET /api/md/candles`, `GET /api/md/quote`); NSE/BSE come only from the connected broker, never Yahoo. Every data failure is shown with its reason. The chart code (`desk/static/chartdesk/`) loads only when the tab opens. The previous chart stays under **Classic chart**. The page's Content-Security-Policy now allows the two crypto WebSocket hosts. CI runs the browser tests (`node --test "tests/js/*.test.mjs"`) and a type check of the chart code (`tsc -p tsconfig.chartdesk.json`).
- **Margin calculator** on the F&O tab: Zerodha's own margin (SPAN, exposure, premium) for any legs of index, stock and MCX futures and options, with the hedge benefit and your funds (`GET /api/margin/underlyings`, `GET /api/margin/contracts`, `POST /api/margin`). With Zerodha, F&O algos check entries against Zerodha's figure instead of the desk's estimate, and **Start** shows the margin needed and warns when funds fall short (`GET /api/fno-algos/{id}/margin`). This uses a `get_basket_margins` tool that `deploy/kite-mcp` adds to kite-mcp-server (Kite Connect's basket margin API). Other brokers and other Kite servers keep the estimate, labelled as such.
- **Real option prices in the option strategy backtest**. Download NSE's daily F&O price files (bhavcopy, both the pre-July-2024 and UDiFF formats) for NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY, MIDCPNIFTY and NIFTYNXT50 into a local SQLite file (resumable; holidays told apart from refusals; through `DESK_EXCHANGE_PROXY` on a server; also `python -m desk.chainhistory`). Positional and signal backtests then fill legs at the option's real open and close, check stops on its own low and high and use the expiries NSE listed. A recorder saves the live option chain from your broker every 5 minutes (near two expiries, ATM ± 20) for intraday backtests. The note under each result counts real and modelled fills.
- **Account funds** on the P&L tab for the account selected at the top. Paper: starting capital, cash available, invested, account value and change since start. Live: your broker's available to trade, used margin, cash (opening balance, added today) and collateral, for Equity and F&O and for Commodity (MCX) (`GET /api/funds?mode=paper|live`).
- **Excel downloads** on the P&L tab. Profit/Loss (closed trades), Order journal, Fills and Tax lots now download as colour-coded .xlsx files: profit in green, loss in red, Buy/Sell in colour, failed orders in red, tax heads shaded, rupee and date formats, frozen header with filters and a totals row that follows the filter. Closed trades add a "By source" sheet (each algo and manual) and tax lots a "By financial year" sheet. Tick **Plain CSV instead** for the old CSV (`GET /api/export/{kind}.xlsx`).
- **size by risk** on the order ticket. Enter a stoploss (and a target) and **Work out quantity** sizes the buy so that the stoploss loses a set share of capital, charges included, capped by capital and the maximum order value, with the reward-to-risk and the win rate it needs (`POST /api/order/size`). New Settings **Risk per trade**: capital for risk, risk %, a refuse-above limit checked on every manual order with a stoploss, and an optional rule that a manual share buy needs a stoploss.
- **trade journal** on the P&L tab. One row per trade (partial exits counted once), with a note for the plan (setup, reason, stoploss, target) and the review (followed the plan, mistakes, lesson); a buy from the ticket saves its plan. Results in R when a stoploss is planned (`GET/POST /api/journal`, `PUT/DELETE /api/journal/{id}`).
- **Your results**: expectancy, win rate, average win and loss, profit factor, expectancy in R, losing streak and largest fall of your own closed trades, the 95% range of the average, a warning under 30 trades, grouped by setup, source, hour, weekday, holding time, side, plan followed or mistake, with the other account for comparison.
- **?** on every tile the desk can explain (P&L, results, backtest): what the number means, worked out with the number shown, and a link to the manual.
- **Can you trust this result?** under every backtest: enough trades, better than the risk-free rate, and 1,000 random orders of the same trades (drawdown to expect, range of final values, chance of a loss). The manual explains survivorship bias in index lists.
- Financial statements in every stock's details, read from the company's own quarterly results filings on NSE (XBRL): quarterly results (12 quarters), profit and loss (years and the last four quarters), balance sheet (March and September), cash flow, ratios (ROE, ROCE, debt to equity, margins, interest cover, debtor and inventory days; bank ratios and NPAs for banks), peers in the same industry, consolidated or standalone. Filings are read once, one a second, and kept on disk (`GET /api/financials`, `POST /api/financials/refresh`).
- Results Drift shows the quarter's sales and profit against a year before (NSE results comparison, or the filings), a Strong / Weak / Mixed verdict, whether the results back the price move, and a "Results back the move" filter.
- Broker choice in Settings → Broker: Upstox, Dhan, Angel One, Fyers and Groww beside Zerodha Kite. The new brokers are experimental: built on each broker's published API and official SDK and covered by offline tests, not yet run against a live account. Kite stays the default.

- Algo details: click an algo (stock or F&O, paper or live) for each stock or leg it traded: buy and sell price, realised and unrealised P&L, Active or Closed, orders still out and every fill.
- Edit an algo that is not running live. While it holds a position only the budget and exit rules can change; the name is fixed; a change of Paper/Live stops it.
- Profit/Loss (closed trades) CSV download.

### Changed

- Sector Compass is now four plain groups (Leading, Losing pace, Lagging, Catching up) decided by two numbers shown for every sector: its return against the Nifty 50 over 3 months and over 4 weeks. The chart is optional, uses the same numbers, and no longer overlaps its labels.
- The P&L tab leads with a plain summary (total, booked, still holding, today) and tables in plain words; statistics and the tax estimate moved under Advanced.
- New TradeSetu logo in the README, the desk sidebar, the sign-in page, the browser tab icon, the installed-app icons and the social preview.

### Fixed
- Backtests watched some stops only at the candle's close while the algo made from them watches the live price: an option strategy's whole-position stoploss and target (₹ and %) and an options buying stoploss and target are now checked inside each candle and filled at their level (or the open, on a gap), so a spike that the close gave back stops the backtest as it would stop the algo. An option strategy driven by a signal no longer opens again at the open of the candle whose stop closed it (it waits for the next candle, as the algo waits for the next day).
- Paper short sale from the chart did nothing: the paper book refused any sale beyond the shares held.
- A chart order's exit (its GTT, stoploss or target order) now closes the trade its entry opened in P&L; before, the exit was booked under its own source and the round trip never showed as a closed trade.
- A live intraday **Sell** from the chart of more than you hold is refused (it was an unchecked short sale that the limits did not count).
- A live delivery (CNC) sell of shares bought the same day was refused as a short sale, because the broker lists them under positions until the next day. The holding check now adds today's CNC buys less today's CNC sells.
- A day-candle backtest trade bought at the open and stopped out the same day paid delivery charges; it now pays intraday charges, as the exchange treats it.

- Algo details: the card said "Orders: None active, every order is closed" while legs were still held, which read as a contradiction. It now reads **Open positions** (how many legs or stocks are held) with **Pending orders** (orders still waiting at the broker) underneath, and a held leg shows **Open** instead of "Active".
- Results Drift showed nothing when BSE refused the server (403). It now retries BSE with cookies, falls back to NSE's event calendar, retries every 30 minutes after a failure, says why in plain words, and can send exchange requests through `DESK_EXCHANGE_PROXY`.
- Stock algos: a sell went ahead when cancelling the algo's target/stoploss GTT failed (the error was ignored), so the GTT could fire later and sell shares that were no longer the algo's. The sell now waits until the GTT is gone; if it has fired, nothing more is sold. A trailing or at-cost stoploss is not moved while the old GTT cannot be cancelled.
- Background jobs (paper GTT check, live fill sync, daily loss limit, F&O algos, paper expiry settlement, MCX square-off, scanner) failed silently. A failure is now logged and alerted, once an hour; an algo that hits an unexpected error says so.
- Daily loss limit: warns which positions it has no price for, instead of leaving them out without a word.
- Paper MCX delivery cut-off: closes the part of a contract held by hand and leaves an algo's lots to the algo (it skipped the whole contract). F&O algo positions are counted per account (paper or live).
- A blocked stock algo's alert no longer repeats its reason.
- F&O algos: an algo could not close its own leg when another algo held the same contract the other way (a condor's sold call that is an iron butterfly's bought wing): the account nets to 0 and the closing buy was refused with "You are short 0". A closing order from an F&O algo is now checked against that algo's own share of the position.
- F&O algos: a block for want of prices said "Log in to Kite" whatever went wrong. It now names what the broker answered (a login needed, too many requests, a timeout), and the blocked alert no longer repeats its reason.
- Option chain: says every listed expiry has passed (refresh the contract list) instead of failing with a server error.
- CI installs the desk extras, so the ~220 desk API tests run instead of skipping. The F&O API tests read contracts on the fake Kite's anchor day, so they no longer fail once the real date passes it.
- A "No edge" badge in Results Drift took the sidebar's style and stretched down the page.
- CSV downloads (order journal, fills, tax lots) failed with "missing or wrong request token": plain links cannot send the request token the API needs. They now download through the API with the token. Text cells a spreadsheet would run as a formula are written as text.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-18

First public release.

### Added
- Trading desk for Indian markets: sector cards, Nifty indices, stock details, charts from 1-minute to daily candles with pivots and indicator panels.
- F&O: option chain, expiries, index futures, 30+ option strategies with payoff charts and backtests.
- MCX gold and silver: futures, options and algos with MCX session, holiday and delivery cut-off rules.
- Strategy builder, built-in and legend strategies, daily and intraday backtests with Zerodha charges, exit rules (stoploss, target, trailing, re-entry) and a walk-forward optimiser.
- Breakout scanner with GTT orders; paper and live algos with risk limits, a daily loss limit and a kill switch.
- P&L, Telegram alerts, tax export, two-factor sign-in and passkeys, installable phone app, Docker/Dokploy hosting.
- Optional AI research agents, derived from TradingAgents (Apache-2.0, see NOTICE).
